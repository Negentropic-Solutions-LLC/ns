Purchasing that always buys from the cheapest supplier

3 vendorsinto one unified catalog & ordering

NetSuite · Data

A custom reordering system, built on your own sales history

60,000+ productseach with its own ordering needs
Fewerstockouts and dead stock

Deciding when to reorder, and how much, is the critical middle ground between empty shelves and cash tied up in dead inventory. I built a custom reorder-point and demand engine from the business's own sales history — accounting for seasonal demand, supplier lead times, and the safety stock each product needs. This gave their purchasing team automated ordering guides for every one of 60,000+ products, across multiple locations.
