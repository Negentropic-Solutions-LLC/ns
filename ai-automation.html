Busy teams, generic tools

Support, communications, operations — the same repetitive tasks keep piling up. Messages to read and sort, information to look up, replies to write. It's the kind of work that's easy to fall behind on and hard to ever fully catch up on.

Off-the-shelf AI tools promise to help, but they're built for everyone, so the output comes out generic and off-brand. To unlock effective use of AI I root that same AI in your own data — your material, your voice, your facts.

What you get

The highest-value opportunities, found first

I start by finding where AI and automation will actually pay off in your workflow — and I'll tell you where they won't. I only build what's worth building.

Repetitive work, handled

The high-volume busy work that eats your team's day — classifying, routing, summarizing — gets automated, so people spend their time on the work that genuinely needs their judgment.

Drafts in your voice, grounded in your facts

AI drafts replies, summaries, and content that sound like your organization and stay true to your real information — not generic filler that someone has to rewrite anyway.

A human approves before anything goes out

I can build review gates at the critical points, so nothing consequential — sending, publishing, or acting — happens without a person's sign-off.

Built on your own organized data

It all runs on a foundation of your data, brought into order first. That's what makes the AI genuinely useful and reliable.
