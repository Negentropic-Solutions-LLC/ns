How an engagement works

Every engagement starts with a free consultation. Before any commitment, I take an honest look at your operation — your systems, your data, and where the friction is — to figure out whether I can deliver real value. If I can't, I'll tell you. If I can, you'll come away with a clear picture of what's possible and what it would take, whether or not you build it with me.
