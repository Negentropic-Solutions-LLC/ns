Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 18, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the internal applications operated by Negentropic Solutions on behalf of its organizational clients, specifically PA Business Insights. These applications are internal tools for authorized users within the client organization. They are not offered as public or consumer services.

Scope of this policy

This policy describes how data is handled when the client organization (Promethean Action) authorizes our applications to access data on its behalf via Google OAuth 2.0. It applies specifically to data received through Google APIs.

Data we access

PA Business Insights uses Google OAuth 2.0 to read a limited set of data from Google services. The data accessed is:

  • YouTube channel metadata — channel ID and title. Used to label per-channel rows in internal revenue reports.
  • YouTube Analytics monetary metrics — estimated revenue, estimated ad revenue, estimated YouTube Premium partner revenue, gross revenue, CPM, and views, at monthly grain. Used to produce per-channel revenue breakdowns for internal business reporting.

The application only accesses data from YouTube channels that the authorizing user owns or manages and explicitly grants access to during the Google OAuth consent flow.

How we use the data

Accessed data is stored in the client organization's private reporting database. It is used solely to produce internal revenue reports, dashboards, and analyses for the client. It is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising, marketing, credit assessment, or any purpose unrelated to the internal reporting function of the application.

Who can access the data

Access is restricted to Negentropic Solutions personnel operating the software on behalf of the client, and to authorized administrators within the client organization. The application runs on infrastructure dedicated to the client and is not shared with third parties.

Retention and deletion

Authorized users can revoke access to the application at any time via their Google Account security settings at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Upon revocation, the application stops accessing further data from the affected account. Any cached data in the client organization's reporting database can be deleted on request by contacting the address listed below.

Security

OAuth credentials and refresh tokens are stored on infrastructure dedicated to the client, with filesystem-level access restrictions (owner-only read permissions). The application runs only on client-controlled infrastructure, not on shared multi-tenant systems.

Google API Services User Data Policy

PA Business Insights' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Changes to this policy

If we update this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.

Contact

Questions about this policy or how data is handled: support@negentropic.llc.
