What we do

Negentropic Solutions LLC is an internet technology and research consultancy. We help organizations connect the tools they already rely on, move and transform their data with care, and surface the insights buried inside it.

The name reflects the work. Negentropy is the tendency toward order — the opposite of entropy. That's what good engineering does for a business: it takes systems pulling in different directions and brings them into coherence.

Services

Custom Integrations

We design and build the connections between your systems — APIs, webhooks, middleware, etc. — so your platforms actually talk to each other instead of working around each other.

Data Engineering

From ingestion to transformation to storage, we build pipelines that are reliable, observable, and maintainable. Clean inputs, dependable outputs.

Data Analysis

We help you ask better questions of your data and get answers you can act on — through reporting, dashboards, ad hoc analysis, and research projects tailored to your domain.
